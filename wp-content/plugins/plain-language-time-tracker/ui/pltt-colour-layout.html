Why joined

Four separate cards read as four unrelated objects. They're not — they're four views of one filtered scope, and joining them says so without a word of explanation. It also removes three borders and four shadows from the top of every screen.

The qualifier lines are kept exactly as they are, including the period comparisons. The only colour change is the unbilled figure moving off red — nothing about money you haven't invoiced yet is an error.

03

Layout change · day grouping

One table, banded. Not nine tables.

Currently each day is its own bordered panel with its own repeated column header. On a month of entries that's twenty panels and twenty header rows saying Description / Tags / Time / Duration. Here the header appears once and days become tinted bands inside a single table.

What moved

The Edit button leaves the day header. It was the only reason the header needed to be a full panel bar, and editing belongs to a row rather than to a date. Day totals take its place on the right, which is information you already compute.

Columns now align down the entire page instead of restarting every day, which is most of why a long month currently feels choppy.
