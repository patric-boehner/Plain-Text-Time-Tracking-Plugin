Billing and Entries

Entries is the working surface. It shows individual entries, filters to any scope, and is where billing actually happens. Billing is an index. It shows billable scopes and bill records, never individual entries, and every row routes into Entries pre-filtered. Neither screen can do the other's job, which is why both exist.

New here: unbilled work is split into ready and waiting. A closed retainer month can be billed today; this month's is still running. Both are unbilled, only one is actionable, and right now they look identical.

Type rule throughout: monospace for what you typed or the clock measured — times, durations, dates, money. Proportional for what the app inferred or you named — clients, projects, descriptions. You can always see which half of a row came from your hands.

Why ready and waiting are separated rather than filtered

Filtering them apart would mean remembering to check the other list. Showing them together, with the waiting rows quiet and stated in terms of what unblocks them — period closes Jul 31 — means the screen answers "what can I bill" and "what's coming" in one look, and you never wonder whether something got lost.

"Amount so far" on a waiting row is deliberately grey: it's a real number, but it will change before you charge it.

What the type rule is doing here

Scan one row: 10:21 – 10:27am, 6m, and $10.00 are monospace — the clock recorded them or they follow arithmetically from what it recorded. "Responding to Daniel's question about security" and "Daniel Mintie · General Website Support" are proportional — you wrote the first and the alias learner matched the second.

It costs nothing, it isn't decorative, and it means the parts of a row you might want to double-check are visually grouped without a single extra label.
