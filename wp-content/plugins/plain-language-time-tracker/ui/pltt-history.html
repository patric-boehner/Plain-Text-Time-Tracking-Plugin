The calendar, if you want it

The only thing a list structurally can't show.

A list can only show days that exist. Days you didn't log aren't rows — they're absent, and absence is invisible in a table. A month grid is the only view where a gap has a shape.

Two things to be careful of

The hatched cells are the whole point, and they're also the risk. A grid of blanks staring back at you is close to a productivity scorecard, which is the thing your principles rule out. Marking only weekdays and never counting a total of missed days keeps it descriptive — it's telling you a log is missing, not that you underperformed.

Second: the shading is hours, so a light cell reads as a weak day. If that grates, the alternative is a single flat tone for "logged" and hatching for "not" — showing presence and absence only, with no intensity at all. That's the safer version and probably the one to build first.
