Element ordering, and the billing elements

Ordering: controls sit above the scope block, because the block is the readout of the controls — its third line states the dates the control produced. Filters never sit between the identity lines and the figures; that splits one object in half.

Billing: the block holds information, a bar beneath it holds action. That separation is what stops the block growing buttons.

01

Vertical order

Same on every screen that filters.
1Title rowPage name, then view toggle, date control, and page-level actions grouped at the right.
2FiltersLabelled dropdowns, applying on selection. Full width. Omitted entirely on screens with no filters.
3Scope blockIdentity and figures — or the light header and stat strip. This is the result of 1 and 2, so it always sits below them.
4Action barOnly when there is something to do with this scope. Attached to the bottom of the block, no gap.
5ChartsOptional, and only where they exist today.
6The tableEverything above narrows or describes it.

Why controls go above and not below the identity

The block's third line reads Showing Feb 17 – May 19, 2026 · 15 entries. That is the answer to the date control, so the control has to precede it. Putting filters between line 3 and the figures would break the block into two panels that look unrelated.

The date control lives in the title row rather than with the filters because it applies on every screen, including ones with no filters at all — keeping it in a fixed position means it's never somewhere new.

Two billing elements, two homes

Existing records are information — a figure (Billed to date $60.00) with the link on its basis line. They don't need a panel; you're not acting on them, you're checking they exist.

Ready to bill is both. The figure states the amount; the bar beneath carries the button. The bar attaches directly to the block with no gap, so it reads as part of the same object rather than as a floating notice.

When nothing is ready, the bar is absent — not present-and-empty. The figure still shows $0.00.

After pressing Review & bill.

Not a new screen. The filter tightens to unbilled, checkboxes appear, and a selection bar takes over. Everything else stays where it was, so you don't lose your place.

What changes between modes

Appears: checkbox column, selection bar. Disappears: Billable, Status, and the row menu — you're not editing here, and Status is redundant when the filter is already "unbilled". Unchanged: title row, filters, scope block, day bands.

Unchecking a row greys it and drops it from the running total, but the amount stays visible so you can see what you're leaving off.

Cancel returns to browsing and restores the Billing filter to its previous value.
