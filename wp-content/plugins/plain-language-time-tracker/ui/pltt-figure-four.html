The rule that emerged

Figure 4's basis line holds only links. Three of four states have nothing to link to, so they have no basis line. The label and the value carry the meaning; the colour separates settled from owed.

Worth noting this is narrower than the basis rule elsewhere — on figures 1–3 the basis line explains the calculation. Figure 4 has no calculation to explain, which is why it ends up empty so often.
