Editing, and getting back to the day

Two jobs were sharing one button. Editing an entry belongs to a row. Reaching the day — its raw log, the journal notes between entries, re-processing — belongs to a date, and it's the thing an entries table currently can't do at all.

So the day band keeps a link, but it navigates rather than edits. The date is the link.

THE ROW → EDIT THE ENTRY

Time, description, client, project, tags, billable. The same modal you have now, opened from the entry it changes rather than from the date it happens to fall on.

THE DATE → GO TO THE DAY

Opens Today at that date: the raw log, the journal lines that never became entries, and the option to re-process. Currently unreachable from Entries.

Two routes to the day, deliberately

The date in the band, and "Go to [date] log" in the row menu. The band is for when you're scanning by day; the menu is for when you're looking at one entry and want the context it came from — which is the more common case when something reads oddly and you want to see the raw line that produced it.

Tags and billable stay inline-editable as they are now. The menu is for the heavier changes.

The line worth stating on screen

"Editing the log does not change entries already recorded." Once processed, the log and the entries are separate — the log is the note you took, the entry is the record you keep. That's already how it works; it's just never said anywhere, which is exactly the kind of thing that makes you hesitate before touching an old log.

Re-process stays available but sits as a secondary action with an ellipsis, since it's the one operation here that can produce duplicates.
