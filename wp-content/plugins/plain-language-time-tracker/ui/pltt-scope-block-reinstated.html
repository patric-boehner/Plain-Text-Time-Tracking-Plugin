Scope block, reinstated

One rule: if the screen is a scope, it gets the full block. A project, a bill, a filtered view — something with terms, and figures measured against those terms. The basis line under each figure is the point, because "$244.50" without "overage only" needs you to remember how retainers work.

If the screen isn't a scope, it gets the light header. Today is a day. It has no terms and its figures need no basis. Same three lines, no block, no basis.

01

Which screens get which

ScreenTreatmentBecause
A projectFull blockHas terms — hours included, fee agreed — and every figure is relative to them.
Entries, filtered to a scopeFull blockThe filter defines a scope with a rate, and the amounts derive from it.
A billFull blockYou're about to charge money against agreed terms. The most consequential screen in the app.
TodayLight headerA day has no terms. Hours and amount need no basis line — they're just what happened.
Entries, unfilteredLight headerNo single scope, so no terms to state. Figures stay in the strip you already have.
Lists — projects, clients, billingLight headerThe scope is every row, not the page.
