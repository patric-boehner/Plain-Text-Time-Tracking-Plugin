How is it billed?
· Hourly
Bill time × rate. Each block is billable unless you say otherwise.
· Monthly retainer
Flat fee for an allocation of hours. Only overage becomes billable.
· Fixed budget
One agreed price. Time is tracked for awareness, not billed by the hour.
· Internal
Your own work. Tracked, never billed, kept out of client reports.
