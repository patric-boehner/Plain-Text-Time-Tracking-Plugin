Why there’s a Process step (and not live parsing)

· Capture first, categorize later. While working you should be able to dump notes without the app reacting or tempting you to stop and tidy. So typing only autosaves — nothing forms below.

· Process is a deliberate “that’s the day” moment. One click parses the whole journal at once and the section below evolves into the confirm step — same page, no jump to a Review screen and back.

· Confirmed blocks lock. Edit the journal and re-process any time; only new lines get parsed, settled blocks are untouched.

Reimagined concept · back to overview · One page, two states: the Process the day section is replaced in place by the blocks to confirm. Click Process the day → to watch it evolve. Unifies Daily Log + Process + Review.
